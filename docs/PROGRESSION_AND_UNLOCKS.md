## 👶 Stage 0: The Cold Start ($1,000 $\rightarrow$ $10,000)

### 1. Minimalist Onboarding
- The player starts with **$1,000 USD** on a single historical stock chart (`NVDA`).
- No complicated tabs, no asset switcher bar, no confusing settings.
- Player learns the core rhythm mechanic:
  - **TAP SPACE**: Reverses position (Bull on top $\leftrightarrow$ Bear on underside).
  - **HOLD SPACE**: Charges lot size from 1 to 100 with escalating vibration and lot badge.
  - **Fog of War**: Navigating the unknown horizon guided by the glowing Bid-Ask spread corridor.

### 2. The Mysterious Benefactor (Uncle Gordon)
The game monitors three parallel conditions during Stage 0:
1. **Time Horizon**: Player survives and trades for **120 seconds** (2 minutes), OR
2. **Profit Run**: Player achieves **$5,000 USD** in total equity, OR
3. **Crisis Event**: Player equity drops to **$100 USD** (near margin call).

**The Encounter**:
- A retro cyber-pager modal intercepts the screen: **Gordon "The Barracuda"** (Senior Managing Partner).
- Narrative dialogue contextualizes the trigger:
  - *Profit*: *"Kid, I've been watching your candlestick runs. You've got instinct—turned pocket change into 5 grand. But trading one lot at a time won't get you off retail streets. I just wired $9,000 into your account..."*
  - *Crisis*: *"Ouch. Almost wiped out your first grand. Welcome to Wall Street. Every legend bleeds on day one. I just wired $9,000 into your broker account so you can stay in the game..."*
  - *Time*: *"Word on the street is you've started surfing the tape. Not bad for a rookie. I just wired $9,000 to give you real capital..."*
- **Unlocks**:
  - Capital increases to **$10,000 USD**.
  - **Multi-Asset Switching Panel (Keys 1–8)** unlocks at the bottom of the screen.

## ⚡ Stage 1: Multi-Asset Apprentice ($10,000 $\rightarrow$ $100,000)

### 1. Asset Rotation & Market Regimes
- Player explores 8 distinct market regimes:
  - `NVDA`: High-momentum parabolic tech volatility.
  - `TSLA`: Violent short squeezes and mean-reverting gaps.
  - `BTC-USD`: Ultra-wide weekend bid-ask spreads and massive trend runs.
  - `SPY`: S&P 500 macro trends, institutional liquidity, tight spreads.
  - `GME`: Historic retail short squeeze mania.
  - `AAPL`: Blue-chip stability and earnings momentum.
  - `AMD`: High-beta semiconductor tracking.
  - `QQQ`: Nasdaq 100 tech-heavy index.

### 2. Flashing Yellow News Alerts ($100,000 Unlock)
- Once the player crosses **$100,000 USD**, the market intelligence feed activates.
- **Microstructure Sensing**:
  - The engine scans 3 to 6 candles ahead into the Fog of War.
  - If a massive price candle ($\ge 2.5\times \text{ATR}$) or volume anomaly is approaching, a **Flashing Yellow Banner** pulses at the top of the viewport:
    - `⚡ BREAKING: MACRO VOLATILITY ANOMALY DETECTED IN FOG OF WAR ⚡`
    - `⚡ BREAKING: EMERGENCY RATE CUT / LIQUIDITY INJECTION IMMINENT ⚡`
    - `⚡ BREAKING: FLASH CRASH CASCADE BREWING 4 CANDLES AHEAD ⚡`
- **Gameplay Effect**: Non-directional warning! The player knows violent turbulence is coming, but must judge whether to hold, charge lots, or prepare to flip!

## 🏢 Stage 3: Independent Prop Desk ($1,000,000 $\rightarrow$ $10,000,000)

### 1. The Complete Trade Console
- When crossing **$1,000,000 USD**, the player unlocks the **Institutional Trade Console**:
  - Split viewport: Autoscroller on left (7/12 width), Trade Execution & Order Book docket on right (5/12 width).
  - Tactical Controls:
    - **`SPACE + ESC` Neutral Cash Flatten**: Closes out 100% of open positions without reversing, allowing players to sit safely in cash during wild consolidation chop.
    - **Time Dilation**: 0.5x Slow-Motion for precision waterfall entries, 2x/5x Fast-Forward for dull range consolidation.

### 2. Terminal Infrastructure Shop
Players reinvest in-game trading profits into execution speed and market access:
- **Colocation Upgrades**:
  - *Tier 1 (Retail Fiber - Free)*: 45ms latency, baseline spread.
  - *Tier 2 (Dedicated Microwave - $150,000)*: 12ms latency, 15% tighter spreads.
  - *Tier 3 (Secaucus NY4 Cross-Connect - $500,000)*: 0.8ms latency, sub-penny execution, zero slippage.
  - *Tier 4 (Chicago CME Microwave Link - $1,200,000)*: 4ms cross-market arbitrage access.
- **Asset Class Licenses**:
  - *Commodities License ($250,000)*: Crude Oil (WTI), Natural Gas, Copper.
  - *Global FX & Rates ($500,000)*: 10Y US Treasury Yields, Japanese Yen carry trades.
  - *Crypto Altcoin Derivatives ($750,000)*: High-leverage perp swaps.

### 3. Execution Venue Routing
- **Lit Exchange (NYSE / NASDAQ)**: Lowest latency, standard taker fee.
- **Dark Pool (CrossFinder / Sigma X)**: Midpoint execution, zero price impact for large block orders (>50 lots), slight fill latency.
- **Wholesale Internalizer (Citadel / Virtu)**: Sub-penny price improvement on retail flow, but subject to adverse selection during trend breakouts.

### 4. The Investment Banking (IB) Distraction Minigames
- Micro-puzzles representing corporate finance busywork appear on the desk console:
  - *Pitchbook Alignment*: Align deal deck valuation multiples under a 15-second timer.
  - *Compliance Audit*: Match suspicious transaction logs before the SEC deadline.
  - *LBO Debt Waterfall*: Route cash flows across senior vs mezzanine tranches.
- **Lucrative Trap**:
  - Solving them pays escalating cash bonuses ($25,000 $\rightarrow$ $150,000 $\rightarrow$ $500,000).
  - **The Danger**: Live market autoscrolling does NOT pause! While your eyes are distracted aligning pitchbook tables, your live positions are moving at full speed, risking catastrophic liquidation.

## 🤖 Stage 4: Quantitative Hedge Fund ($10,000,000 $\rightarrow$ $100,000,000)

### 1. Interactive Bot Studio & Delayed Stream
- At $10M, biological manual trading cannot keep up with multi-venue speeds.
- Player designs automated trading algorithms using modular signal blocks:
  - **Signal Nodes**: EMA crosses, RSI divergences, Bollinger squeezes, Volume Delta bursts.
  - **Cross-Asset Logic**: *"If Gold drops >1% and Dollar Index spikes, short EUR/USD 50 lots."*
- **15-Minute Delayed Live Stream**:
  - Feeds public market data into custom bots.
  - Allows players to let their fund run **idly in the background**, generating systematic capital while they analyze higher-order strategies.

### 2. Creditor Restructuring (Margin Call Redefined)
- Post-$10M, margin breach no longer results in instant Game Over.
- Catastrophic drawdowns trigger **Chapter 11 Creditor Restructuring**:
  - Creditors inject rescue capital into a single emergency pool.
  - Imposes compounding daily **interest debt schedules** (SOFR + 650 bps).
  - Consecutive breaches compound interest rates aggressively, requiring high-stakes turnaround trading to service the debt and regain equity autonomy.

## 🏛️ Stage 5: Institutional Sovereign ($100,000,000+)

### 1. Too Big To Fail
- Margin calls are permanently eliminated.
- Unlocks **Central Bank & Sovereign Lines of Credit**:
  - Access to primary dealer credit facilities ($400,000,000 capacity).
  - The player operates not as a retail gambler, but as a **systemic market maker** shaping market liquidity.

### 2. Level 2 / Level 3 Microstructure Simulation
- Full Depth of Market (DOM) order book simulation:
  - Visualizing order book walls, iceberg orders, and spoofing liquidity.
  - Simulating solvency cascades: large market orders devour liquidity depth, creating cascading liquidation slippage across venues.

### 3. Private Equity (PE) & OTC Derivatives
- **Private Equity**:
  - Acquire distressed telecom vendors, exchange data feeds, and clearinghouses to permanently lower operational costs and earn recurring EBITDA dividends.
- **Over-The-Counter (OTC) Desk**:
  - Structure bespoke bilateral contracts (variance swaps, barrier options, catastrophe bonds) with institutional counterparties.
